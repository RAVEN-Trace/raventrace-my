## Publication policy

For material news stories, RAVEN-Trace prefers a real photograph with clear reuse rights when it is contextually accurate. If no suitable licensed photograph is available, the fallback is a clearly labelled RAVEN editorial news card. Synthetic images of identifiable people are not used as evidence photography.
