## NLP / narrative safeguards live

- **SELECTION EFFECT** — true facts can still create a distorted picture when material context is omitted.
- **CATEGORY COLLAPSE** — loss, asset-liability gap, impairment, questionable transaction and theft are not interchangeable categories.
- **EMOTIONAL PRIMING** — identity or emotionally loaded wording can shape interpretation before evidence is processed.
- **MOTIVE INFLATION** — observable actions do not establish hidden intention without separate evidence.
- **REPETITION RISK** — misleading slogans should not be repeated more than necessary.
- **REPRESENTATIVENESS ERROR** — viral posts do not establish population or platform-wide sentiment without auditable sampling.
- **NUMBER LAUNDERING** — a large transaction/investment figure must not be allowed to drift into an alleged personal-benefit amount without evidentiary support.

These are analytical risk labels, not findings that a named actor intentionally manipulated the public.
