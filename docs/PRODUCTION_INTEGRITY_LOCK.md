## Narrative Forensics V4.2 — editorial lock

The Narrative section is no longer treated primarily as a political-map page. Its editorial function is now **Narrative Forensics**: identify what a story asks the public to believe, separate the verified record from interpretation, expose framing mechanisms, recover omitted context, and reconstruct the narrowest explanation the evidence can carry.

Core sequence:

1. **FAKTA ASAS** — what the auditable record actually establishes.
2. **DAKWAAN** — what a speaker, post or political actor asks the audience to believe.
3. **TEKNIK NARATIF** — language, category mixing, selective presentation, identity cues, emotional priming, repetition or motive attribution.
4. **KONTEKS HILANG** — material facts needed before the claim can responsibly be evaluated.
5. **REKONSTRUKSI** — the most accurate public explanation supportable by current evidence.

The locked public-language rule is: **neutralise, bukan counter-spin**. Raven does not answer one slogan with another. It begins with the verified model, names manipulation techniques only where supported, avoids unnecessary repetition of misleading claims, and returns readers to an evidence-based reconstruction.

## NLP / narrative safeguards now live

The public Narrative Forensics layer explicitly identifies the following manipulation/error pathways without treating the labels themselves as proof of deceptive intent:

- **SELECTION EFFECT** — accurate facts can still create a distorted picture when material context is selectively omitted.
- **CATEGORY COLLAPSE** — distinct concepts such as loss, asset-liability gap, impairment, questionable transaction and theft must not be collapsed into one criminal-sounding category.
- **EMOTIONAL PRIMING** — identity or emotionally loaded wording can shape interpretation before the evidence is processed.
- **MOTIVE INFLATION** — observable actions do not establish hidden intention without separate evidence.
- **REPETITION RISK** — misleading slogans should not be repeated more than necessary; correction should lead and close with the accurate model.
- **REPRESENTATIVENESS ERROR** — viral or repeated posts do not establish population or platform-wide sentiment without auditable sampling.

These are analytical risk labels, not findings that a named political actor intentionally manipulated the public.
