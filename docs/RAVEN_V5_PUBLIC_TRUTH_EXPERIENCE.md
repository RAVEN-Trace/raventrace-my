# RAVEN V5 — PUBLIC TRUTH EXPERIENCE

**Status:** ACTIVE DESIGN DOCTRINE

Raven is not only an investigative publication. Raven is a public truth-navigation system.

The product goal is simple:

> Help ordinary people understand what happened, what is proven, what is still only a claim, what context is missing, and how a narrative may be shaping their conclusion.

## Audience

Raven is designed first for ordinary Malaysian readers:

- makcik / pakcik
- teenagers
- people who do not follow politics every day
- readers without legal, accounting or investigative backgrounds
- mobile visitors arriving from Facebook, Threads or shared links

Do not require the reader to become an expert before they can understand the story.

## Core rule

**Complex investigation. Simple explanation.**

Simplify language, not evidence.

Internal Raven may use legal, forensic, accounting and analytical terminology. Public Raven explains the meaning first, then exposes the technical term only when useful.

## Voice

Raven should sound like a smart investigative journalist sitting next to the reader and explaining a complicated issue in normal Malaysian language.

Not:

- a government press release
- a lawyer's submission
- a university lecture
- an analyst dashboard
- a hyperactive social-media account

Target voice:

**smart · curious · slightly cheeky · calm · very clear**

Useful patterns:

- “Okay. Apa yang kita tahu setakat ni?”
- “Nampak macam sama kan? Sebenarnya tak.”
- “Ini part yang ramai orang terlepas.”
- “Viral? Ya. Wakil seluruh Malaysia? Belum tentu.”
- “Internet suka shortcut. Mahkamah tak boleh.”

Humour must never trivialise victims, religion, allegations, criminal proceedings or serious harm.

## Editorial doctrine

1. **Bukti dahulu. Kesimpulan kemudian.**
2. **Neutralise, bukan counter-spin.**
3. **Bahasa mudah. Bukti kekal tepat.**
4. **Do not fight people. Fight distortion.**
5. **Do not ask readers to trust Raven blindly. Show the record.**

## Narrative Forensics

Internal analytical sequence:

1. FAKTA ASAS
2. DAKWAAN
3. TEKNIK NARATIF
4. KONTEKS HILANG
5. REKONSTRUKSI

Public translation:

- **Cerita dia apa?**
- **Apa yang betul?**
- **Apa yang cerita ni tak sebut?**
- **Trick dia kat mana?**
- **Lepas buang framing, apa yang tinggal?**

Narrative mechanisms may be labelled internally, but public explanations lead with human language:

| Internal mechanism | Public-facing language |
| --- | --- |
| Selection Effect | Dia tunjuk yang ini je. |
| Category Collapse | Eh, benda ni tak sama. |
| Emotional Priming | Dia bagi kita rasa dulu. |
| Motive Inflation | Kita nampak tindakan. Niat? Belum tentu. |
| Repetition Risk | Ulang banyak kali tak jadikan benda tu betul. |
| Representativeness Error | Viral tak semestinya suara semua orang. |
| Technical Fog | Ayat susah tak semestinya ayat betul. |

These labels describe possible effects or risks. They do **not** prove deceptive intent by a named actor.

## Public evidence labels

Canonical evidence states remain strict. Public UI may add a human translation:

- **FACT** → Memang disahkan
- **CLAIM** → Ada orang kata
- **DISPUTED** → Rekod tak sama
- **UNKNOWN** → Belum tahu lagi
- **INFERENCE / CONTEXT** → Masuk akal / perlu konteks, tapi belum terbukti
- **PROCESS** → Proses masih jalan

Never visually present an allegation with the same certainty as a verified fact.

## Information architecture

Raven pages should answer these questions in this order whenever practical:

1. Apa benda ni?
2. Apa berlaku?
3. Kenapa orang kecoh?
4. Apa yang kita tahu betul-betul?
5. Apa yang belum terbukti?
6. Apa narrative yang sedang dibawa?
7. Apa benda penting yang tak disebut?
8. Nak check sendiri? → sumber / dokumen / timeline

Technical depth remains available one layer deeper.

## Page roles

### Homepage

A **truth navigator**, not a case dashboard.

The reader should quickly choose:

- Aku nak faham dulu
- Apa cerita yang tengah dimainkan?
- Aku nak tengok bukti sendiri

### Investigations hub

A case chooser. Future investigations must be able to sit beside RCI Tabung Haji without redesigning the information architecture.

### CASEFILE

Two layers:

**Public understanding layer**
- briefing
- what changed
- who / status
- narrative check
- what remains unknown

**Deep evidence layer**
- timeline
- money
- governance
- source room
- documents
- methodology

### Narrative Forensics

A visual reasoning experience. Show the claim, verified record, missing context, framing mechanism and reconstruction. Avoid walls of analyst terminology.

### Newsroom

Not merely “latest news”. Organise around:

- apa berubah
- apa tak berubah
- apa masih belum tahu
- apa claim baru yang perlu disemak

### Methodology

Lead with “Macam mana Raven check benda ni?” before detailed protocol language.

### Corrections

Lead with accountability:

> Raven boleh silap. Kat sini kami betulkan.

Show what changed, why, and whether the conclusion changed.

### Tips

Lead with ordinary-reader language while keeping safety warnings precise.

### About

Explain why Raven exists before explaining organisation structure.

## Visual system

Raven remains visually credible, but V5 reduces institutional stiffness.

- Dark surfaces = orientation / investigation identity
- Paper/light surfaces = reading and understanding
- Cyan = evidence/action/navigation
- Amber = caution / incomplete state
- Red = disputed/high-risk, never decorative alarm
- Serif = selective long-form/editorial use
- Sans = explanation and public interface
- Mono = source IDs / forensic metadata only

Avoid:

- crypto dashboard aesthetics
- hacker UI
- card walls
- excessive borders
- too much mono text
- decorative data visualisation
- permanent marketing-style CTAs
- formal bureaucratic visual language

## Visualisation rule

Use a visual only when it reduces cognitive load.

Prefer simple public explanations such as:

**14 pelaburan disyor diperiksa**

≠

**14 kertas siasatan SPRM**

≠

**14 kes jenayah terbukti**

Do not use a graph simply because a graph looks impressive.

## Interaction

Useful interactions answer a reader question:

- Apa maksud ni?
- Kenapa penting?
- Apa bukti?
- Apa yang belum tahu?
- Apa yang cerita ni tinggalkan?

Interactions that exist only for visual spectacle should be removed.

## Mobile-first rule

Primary public design target: **360–430px**.

External social readers are likely to arrive on mobile. Design the information architecture for mobile deliberately.

Prioritise:

- one clear idea at a time
- short chunks
- readable typography
- strong tap targets
- small number of choices
- contextual next steps
- no giant card walls
- no desktop layout merely collapsed into one column

## Raven Kedai Kopi Test

Before approving public copy or an interface block, ask:

- Does this sound like a government statement?
- Does this sound like a university lecture?
- Would a Form 2 student need three more paragraphs to understand it?
- Does the visual look intelligent but make understanding harder?

If yes: rewrite or redesign.

Target reaction:

> “Ohhh… macam tu rupanya.”

## Success condition

A first-time social visitor should be able to:

- identify the issue within about 5–10 seconds
- understand the claim-vs-evidence boundary within about 30 seconds
- reach deeper evidence without getting lost
- leave feeling more capable of evaluating the narrative themselves

The final goal is not simply pageviews.

The goal is to reduce the gap between public perception and what the evidence actually supports.
